# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Elocutio to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or writing ability. We pledge to make participation harassment-free and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and specific. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions from newcomers; "read the docs" is not an answer.
- Respect differing opinions about product scope, writing style, and technology.
- Give credit for ideas and contributions.
- Keep discussions on-topic and in public channels unless privacy is required.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery; unwelcome attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Dismissing someone's writing, language, or accent as a proxy for ability.
- Using the project to build tools for plagiarism, exam cheating, impersonation, or harassment.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events, and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers via GitHub (open a confidential issue to `@pri-te-sh`) or email `conduct@elocutio.app` once the domain mailbox is configured. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers. Consequences may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.

Maintainers who violate this code are subject to the same consequences.
