Get Started

Build and run on macOS.

Elocutio is in pre-alpha (M1 in progress). Clone the repository to build the menu-bar companion and shared Rust workspace from source.

Why is Elocutio distributed outside the Mac App Store?

Reading and replacing text across other apps requires the macOS Accessibility API, which the App Sandbox does not permit. Release builds target a Developer ID–signed, Apple-notarized DMG and Homebrew cask.

What leaves my Mac when I use an LLM provider?

In local-only mode, nothing leaves your Mac. With a configured provider, only the text being analyzed (plus your compact Style Card if you turn on Use my voice) is sent directly to that provider using the key in your macOS Keychain.

What is explicitly out of scope for v1?

Google Docs (canvas-rendered; Gmail, Notion, Slack, and Linear are covered by the planned browser extension), plagiarism scores, AI-detector scores, and enterprise SSO. Windows, iOS, and Android follow the macOS release on the shared Rust core.

Local Voice Style Card (elocutio-voice)

How Local Voice Learning Works

1. Local Feature Extraction: When you paste a writing sample in the Voice DNA tab, elocutio-voice computes sentence cadence, formality register, punctuation habits, and verb preferences in memory on your Mac and discards the raw text.

2. Accepted & Dismissed Edit Telemetry: When Learn from edits is enabled, accepted and dismissed suggestions in elocutio.sqlite3 refine your active traits without sending samples to any server.

3. 8-Gram Copy Guard: Every AI rewrite is checked against an 8-gram overlap filter before being shown, preventing any model from repeating phrases from your samples verbatim.
