How it works

An editor, not a grammar cop.

Fast local checks handle the obvious. Your model handles judgment. Every suggestion is an offer you can take or leave.

01

Catch

Spelling, grammar, and punctuation are checked on your Mac in under 10 ms. No network, no waiting.

We recieved the files and will make a decision on it today.
Local rules · 3 ms · offline
02

Explain

Each suggestion says what changed and why. Apply it, dismiss it, or add the word to your lexicon so it never comes up again.

“make a decision on” → “decide”
A verb does the same work in fewer words.
03

Rewrite

Select any text and press ⌥⌘E. Your chosen model reshapes it for clarity or tone, and in your voice if you switch that on.

Polish flowTightenConfidentWarmExecutive

Voice learning

It learns how you write, and shows you what it learned.

Paste a few samples or let it learn from the edits you accept. Elocutio builds a short, readable style card: your rhythm, your register, the words you reach for and the ones you avoid. Rewrites follow it. Nothing is fine-tuned.

  • Both switches are off until you turn them on.
  • Samples are analyzed on your Mac and discarded. Only the style card is kept.
  • A copy guard refuses any rewrite that repeats your samples back to you.
  • Delete the profile and everything it learned goes with it.

Privacy

An app, not a service.

There is no Elocutio account and no Elocutio server. The only network requests are the ones you set up.

Your text goes where you send it

To the provider you picked, when you ask. In local mode, nowhere at all.

Keys stay on your Mac

Stored in the macOS Keychain and encrypted in a local database.

No telemetry

No analytics, device IDs, or keystroke logging. Crash reports are opt-in.

Nothing trains on you

Your writing is never used to fine-tune a model, ours or anyone’s.

Every request is logged, for you

A local network log shows host, model, tokens, and cost. Never your text.

Get started

Build it on your Mac.

Four commands. The app runs from your menu bar and asks for Accessibility permission so it can read the field you’re writing in.

  • macOS14 Sonoma or later
  • ToolchainXcode CLT, Rust, Node 22+, pnpm
  • A modelAn API key, or a local model

Builds are unsigned until v1. On first launch, right-click the app and choose Open.

Terminal 
$ git clone https://github.com/pri-te-sh/elocutio.git
$ cd elocutio
$ ./scripts/bootstrap.sh
$ just macos-dev
